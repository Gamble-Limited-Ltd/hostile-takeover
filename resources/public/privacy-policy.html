What We Do NOT Collect.

We do not store replays or the move-by-move record of any game, payment information, phone numbers, location data, dates of birth, advertising identifiers or device fingerprints. Your age is established only by the confirmation you give at sign-up.

§ 2 — How We Use Your Data

Account Management. Your username and password hash let you sign in. Your session records keep you signed in. Your rank decides whether your account has admin tools.

Eligibility. Your email address is used only to check that it ends in @kmlhs.org. It is never verified, never emailed and never shown to other players.

Identifying Players. Your first and last name let the admin know which friend owns which account. They are not shown to other players.

Gameplay. Your in-match name, chat and emoji are shown to the other players in that match, and your actions are relayed to them so the game can run.

Progression and Profiles. Your game results earn XP and levels. Every account has a public profile showing its username, flag, level, XP, wins and games played per mode, and the date it was created. The same details appear on the leaderboards and next to your name in lobbies and matches. Anyone who opens the game can see them. Your first and last name and your email address are never shown.

Security. IP addresses are used to limit repeated sign-in attempts. Session records, user-agents and admin audit records help us spot and investigate misuse of accounts. Server logs are used to diagnose problems.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, selling or any purpose other than running the game.

§ 3 — Data Sharing

Gamble Limited Ltd. does not sell, rent, trade or share your personal data with anyone for any commercial purpose. We do not use advertising networks, marketing partners, data brokers or tracking pixels.

We rely on one service provider to deliver the platform:

  • Cloudflare (network and hosting infrastructure). All traffic to the platform passes through Cloudflare, which terminates encrypted connections on our behalf. It necessarily processes your IP address and request content in transit to deliver and protect the site. We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views and measures page load times without cookies and without tracking you across other websites. We use no other analytics service. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Email. If you email our support address, your message is handled by our email provider.

We will disclose data only if required by law, valid legal process or a request from law enforcement.

§ 4 — Data Retention

  • Account data (name, email, username, password hash, age confirmation, rank): kept while your account exists; deleted when the account is deleted.
  • Sessions: a session stops working 30 days after it was last renewed, or as soon as you sign out. Records of ended sessions are kept with your account and deleted when the account is deleted.
  • Password reset links: kept with your account and deleted when the account is deleted.
  • Game results, XP, level and profile flag: kept while your account exists; your results, XP, level and flag are deleted when the account is deleted. A game's general details (mode, map, time and length) stay, with nothing linking them to you.
  • In-match names, chat and emoji: discarded when the match ends.
  • Server log files (which can include IP addresses): size-capped and overwritten as new entries are written. They are an operational record of the platform and are not edited as part of account deletion.
  • Administrative audit records: kept after account deletion for platform integrity. They contain the usernames involved (for a rename, the old and new name) and, for an XP fix, the level and XP before and after, but no other account data.
  • Backups: daily database backups are kept for 14 days, so deleted data can remain in a backup for up to 14 days before it is gone permanently.

§ 5 — User Rights

Right to Deletion. You can permanently delete your account at any time with Delete account in the game, which takes effect immediately. You can also ask us to delete it (see § 9); we will do so within thirty (30) days of your request. Deleted accounts cannot be recovered.

Right to Access. You may ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you. We will provide it within thirty (30) days of your request.

Right to Correction. If any data we hold about you is wrong, ask us and we will correct it within thirty (30) days.

§ 6 — Children

Hostile Takeover is for people aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we will delete it. If you believe this has happened, contact us (see § 9).

§ 7 — Security

Password Storage. Passwords are stored only as argon2id hashes. Your password is sent to us over an encrypted connection, checked against the stored hash and then discarded. It is never written to storage or logs.

Session Management. Your session is kept in a single HTTP-only cookie that page scripts cannot read. We store only a one-way hash of its token. The short-lived sign-in tokens the game uses are digitally signed with a key held only on our server. Changing your password signs out every other session on your account.

Transport Security. All connections to the platform use HTTPS (TLS).

Administrative Access. Admin tools are available only to the admin account, and every admin action is recorded in the audit records described in § 1.

§ 8 — Cookies

Session Cookie. We use one cookie, ht_refresh, to keep you signed in. It is strictly necessary: without it you would be signed out.

No Tracking or Advertising Cookies. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies or tracking pixels, and we do not track you across other websites. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies.

§ 9 — Changes and Contact

Changes. We may update this Privacy Policy. Before we collect any new kind of data (for example, if we ever start saving replays), we will update this policy and its effective date and tell players in the game.
